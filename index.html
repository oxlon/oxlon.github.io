    Expertise

    What we work on

    Our work spans ten disciplines across three practice groups. Select any discipline to inspect its live model and equations in the workbench above.

    Economics and Finance

    1. I

      Forecasting

      Forecasting systems for inflation, output, labour markets and asset prices, from nowcasts to full predictive distributions.

      Nowcasting · Density Forecasts
    2. II

      Macroeconomic Analysis

      Joint models of prices, employment and output translated into structured scenarios for policy and institutional planning.

      VAR & BVAR · Scenarios
    3. III

      Monetary Policy

      Empirical analysis of central bank decisions, monetary transmission channels and central bank communication.

      Policy Rates · Text
    4. IV

      Economic and Financial Analysis

      Bond markets, term-structure modelling, asset pricing, competition economics and empirical market structure.

      Yield Curves · Regulation
    5. V

      Risk and Volatility

      Quantitative measurement of financial risk, from market volatility regimes and tail events to institutional stress tests.

      Tail Risk · Stress Testing

    AI and Data

    1. VI

      Machine Learning

      Supervised and unsupervised models that uncover structure in large economic, financial and administrative datasets.

      Classification · Text as Data
    2. VII

      Deep Learning

      Design, training and deployment of neural architectures for time series, documents, language and visual data.

      Language Models · Document AI
    3. VIII

      AI and the New Economy

      Empirical research on how AI reshapes occupations, firms, productivity and the economics of compute.

      AI Adoption · Compute

    Mathematics and Computing

    1. IX

      Mathematics

      Dynamical systems, optimisation, probability theory and numerical analysis underpinning every model we build.

      Optimisation · Numerical Methods
    2. X

      Computing Infrastructure

      Data pipelines, GPU computing and private AI systems engineered to run on an institution's own servers.

      Data Pipelines · On-Premise AI
