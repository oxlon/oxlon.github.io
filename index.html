      What We Work On

      Our work covers ten areas in three groups. Each area has a live example at the top of the page, built with the same methods we use in our projects.

      Economics and Finance

      1. I

        Forecasting

        We build forecasting systems for inflation, output, labour markets and asset prices, from nowcasts to full predictive distributions.

      2. II

        Macroeconomic Analysis

        We model how prices, jobs and output move together and turn the models into scenarios for policy and planning.

      3. III

        Monetary Policy

        We analyse central bank decisions, how they pass through to the economy and how central banks communicate them.

      4. IV

        Economic and Financial Analysis

        We analyse bond markets, asset prices and the economy behind them, from term-structure models to competition and market structure.

      5. V

        Risk and Volatility

        We measure financial risk, from market volatility and tail events to stress tests.

      AI and Data

      1. VI

        Machine Learning

        We train models that find structure in large economic, financial and administrative datasets, with or without labels.

      2. VII

        Deep Learning

        We design, train and deploy neural networks for time series, text, documents and images.

      3. VIII

        AI and the New Economy

        We study how AI changes work, firms and productivity and what its compute costs.

      Mathematics and Computing

      1. IX

        Mathematics

        Dynamical systems, optimisation, probability and numerical analysis sit under every model we build.

      2. X

        Computing Infrastructure

        We build data pipelines, GPU computing and AI systems that run on an institution's own servers.
