Exchange rates against the US dollar and the euro

FR10 · notebook FR10.ipynb · statutory sheet 2.4.1.5.-7.

#1Requirement (Tələb)

AZ, verbatim (MİİS TT §15.5.1, FR10): "Milli valyutanın ABŞ dollarına və avroya nisbətdə orta illik məzənnəsinin təhlili və proqnozlaşdırılması."

English: "Analysis and forecasting of the average annual exchange rate of the national currency against the US dollar and the euro."

Source: MİİS_TT_.pdf, pp. 36–40. Legal basis: Cabinet of Ministers decision No. 75 of 24.05.2004, clause 2.4.1.

Two series on one definition: the average annual manat rate against the dollar and the euro. Neither is estimated elsewhere in the package and almost every other requirement consumes them, so this stage runs near the top of the execution order.

#3Model and why it was chosen (Model və seçim səbəbi)

The manat has been fixed against the dollar since 2017: a declared regime, not a behavioural relation, so the horizon path is set as a regime assumption and its skill against the random walk is measured rather than asserted. The euro rate is arithmetically the EUR/USD cross, and two readings were raced — peg arithmetic and the random-walk family. On the full window the peg rule loses heavily, because applying the current parity to pre-2017 years produces a large systematic error; on the peg sub-window the three-year cross rule beats the benchmark from four different start years, and the central path is built from those sub-window weights, since the horizon is conditional on the regime continuing.

#4Econometric approach (Ekonometrik yanaşma)

AZN/EURt=1.70×(EUR/USD)t\text{AZN/EUR}_t = 1.70 \times (\text{EUR/USD})_tAZN/EURt​=1.70×(EUR/USD)t​

No behavioural equation is estimated here and this requirement writes no row to the equation catalogue: the published quantity is a path rule plus its test result. The unit-root test supports that — the euro level does not reject a unit root, its difference is stationary. The band is not calibrated from the literature: the annual sigma is estimated from the actual series over the peg period; a full-sample sigma would be about twice as large, because it contains the devaluation.

Coverage and price basis are rendered from series_dictionary.csv at build time.

Two changes in this release. First, an accounting correction: the specification asks for the average annual rate, and the previous release estimated and published on the end-of-period row. The two coincide under the peg but not before it — in 2015 the end-of-period rate reads 1.56 against an average of 1.03 — so estimation, testing and publication now run on the average annual series. Second, the external triangulation is retired: the euro rate is no longer computed from an outside EUR/USD reference, because the central bank publishes the actual average annual series, cross-checked against the statistical office's implicit rates. The template's own rows for both series return #REF! — a broken reference, not a missing series.

#7Reading (Şərh)

The dollar path is flat by assumption, and the horizon is conditional on that assumption holding; the 2015–2016 precedent shows it is a policy decision, not a statistical property. The euro path moves only with the cross rate, from 1.87 to 1.88 AZN/EUR, with a band from the measured volatility of the peg period; it is exposed twice, through the manat-dollar link and through the cross.

#9Reproduce (Təkrar istehsal)

To obtain these results again, from the root of the delivery package:

cd 3_model
python3 run_all.py --stage FR10

Notebook: 3_model/notebooks/FR10.ipynb. Inputs: the central bank monetary-indicators workbook (table 2.16), the statistical office cross-check table, the Ministry workbook (Monetar sektoru). Written: the usd_azn and eur_azn assumption keys, 2_neticeler/forecast_long.csv, 2_neticeler/validation_backtest.csv. Runtime: about 3–6 seconds for this notebook, about 85 seconds for the full pipeline.
