Foreign trade in goods and services

FR7 · notebook FR07.ipynb · statutory sheet 2.4.1.13.-14.

#1Requirement (Tələb)

AZ, verbatim (MİİS TT §15.5.1, FR7): "Mal və xidmətlərin idxal-ixracının əsas göstəriciləri, strukturu, tədiyə balansının proqnozlaşdırılması."

English: "Forecasting the main indicators and the structure of exports and imports of goods and services, and the balance of payments."

Source: MİİS_TT_.pdf, pp. 36–40. Legal basis: Cabinet of Ministers decision No. 75 of 24.05.2004, clause 2.4.1.

The clause asks for three things at once: the main goods and services flows on the balance of payments definition (BPM6), their oil-gas / non-oil structure, and balances that close as identities.

#3Model and why it was chosen (Model və seçim səbəbi)

Oil-gas exports are built as price × volume, because the two halves are driven from different places and can be checked separately: the price against Brent, the volume from the exogenous production plan. The other four flows are estimated in log differences, since the level series are non-stationary and the sample is too short for level relationships. The real effective exchange rate was tested in both goods equations and kept in one: in the non-oil export equation its coefficient came out positive — real appreciation raising exports — which measures the shared oil cycle rather than competitiveness, so that variant stays in the catalogue and does not reach the forecast. Oil-gas imports receive no behavioural equation, because no candidate beat the random walk; the benchmark carries the path and the item is held flat, and balances stay identities.

Long-run relations are tested only with the extended Engle–Granger procedure (MacKinnon critical values). It confirms cointegration between the export price and Brent — hence the error-correction form — and rejects it between export value and Brent, which is why value is decomposed: EG-coint[ln P^export ~ ln Brent] stat=-5.147 p=0.0001 n=29 k=1 critical(1%: -4.33, 10%: -3.20, 5%: -3.56) → cointegrated; EG-coint[ln X^oil ~ ln Brent] stat=-3.126 p=0.0833 n=16 k=1 critical(1%: -4.78, 10%: -3.34, 5%: -3.77) → no cointegration. Break dummies are not added to the goods equations: an extra parameter would exhaust the degrees of freedom.

#7Reading (Şərh)

Oil-gas exports drift down while non-oil imports rise with domestic absorption, so the trade surplus falls from 5,062 to 1,604 mln USD without turning negative on the central path. The gap to the template sample is an assumption difference: it implies a lower export price. The services balance stays in deficit throughout, and the balance bands are wide by construction — the direction is the usable part of this table, not the final-year level.

#8Backtest (Geriyə Sınaq)

Expanding-window, one-step-ahead, out of sample, against a random walk; structural models see the current year's driver values, as they will in the pipeline.

The rival column carries the negative rows: on oil-gas imports every candidate scored below the benchmark, and on services exports the partner-growth-only equation did too. The bottom-up sum of the six service components was raced against the aggregate equation and joined the ensemble in both directions; the residual between sub-lines and total is itself a series, exp_serv_uzlasma (0.898 in the final year).
