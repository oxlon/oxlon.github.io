Product output in physical units

FR4 · notebook FR04.ipynb · statutory sheet 2.4.1.4.

#Requirement (Tələb)

AZ, verbatim (MİİS TT §15.5.1, FR4): "Əsas məhsul növlərinin sahələr üzrə natural göstəricilərinin proqnozlaşdırılması (o cümlədən aksizli məhsullar)."

English: "Forecasting the physical-unit indicators of the main product types by sector (including excisable products)."

Source: MİİS_TT_.pdf, pp. 36–40. Legal basis: Cabinet of Ministers decision No. 75 of 24.05.2004, clause 2.4.1.

The quantity asked for is physical output, not a value aggregate. The parenthesis does not create a separate list: excisable goods sit inside the same catalogue, in the drink, tobacco and petroleum-product subsections.

#Model and why it was chosen (Model və seçim səbəbi)

The hard problem here is the integrity of the statutory sheet, not the econometrics. Ten product names repeat across rows, so products are keyed by the composite (sheet, row, unit) rather than by name. 272 cells on the sheet return #REF! across 28 rows, and one row's own name is #REF!; none is read as zero, and each is documented in vereq_ref_cells.csv.

Each product takes one of three methods, decided in code:

MethodRuleCount
STRUKTURdriver coefficient positive with p below 0.10, and the implied average growth economically plausible31
ETALONgate failed or the plausibility check reversed it — capped median growth over the recent window126
SABİTtoo few usable observations — flat line from the last real value, with a data-gap flag21

The driver is assigned from the product's section code, not by hand, out of four paths estimated upstream: oil-gas real growth, non-oil growth, core investment growth, core consumption growth. Every cap event and plausibility veto is printed, and the exported path is built from the capped value, so equation and shipped series cannot disagree.

The worst frozen block was repaired. Eleven food rows had been #REF!-dead for years and were flat lines; ten were revived on the statistical office's industry and agriculture tables, taking dynamics only, rebased to the template's anchor year, with a constant-ratio test on the overlap as the acceptance criterion. Fruit and vegetable juices was deliberately not revived — template in thousand decalitres against a statistical table in thousand tonnes, with a ratio that moves across the overlap — so what is missing there is a unit reconciliation, not a concordance. The row with a broken name is excluded entirely.

#Econometric approach (Ekonometrik yanaşma)

Running ADF separately on 179 short annual level series would not be informative, so non-stationarity is shown on two representative series and the log-difference specification is then applied universally, with HAC standard errors.

#Reading (Şərh)

The products with a real economic channel are the oil-gas chain, where the driver is itself a modelled volume index. Everywhere else the forecast is a capped median rule — the review's own objection — and for most products the honest description is a disciplined extrapolation, not a behavioural model. The revived food rows are the measurable improvement here: frozen lines became estimated paths with a backtest number attached.

#Backtest (Geriyə Sınaq)

Testing all 179 rows would add no information: every series has the same short window. The table carries two published panels from validation_backtest.csv — a twelve-product representative panel spanning all six sections, and the ten revived food rows tested as STRUKTUR and capped-median against the random walk.

Reading: the driver beats the benchmark for the oil-gas chain (+55% for crude oil) and loses for cement (−1093%), consistent with cement failing the gate. All ten revived rows beat the random walk by a modest margin.
