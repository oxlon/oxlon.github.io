Household income, expenditure, wages and employment

FR8 · notebook FR08.ipynb · statutory sheet 2.4.1.5.-7.

English: "Analysis and forecasting of the population's money incomes, expenditures and their growth rates."

Source: MİİS_TT_.pdf, pp. 36–40. Legal basis: Cabinet of Ministers decision No. 75 of 24.05.2004, clause 2.4.1.

One heading covers five connected blocks: household income and expenditure in levels and real growth, the average monthly wage, employment and unemployment, and paid services.

#3Model and why it was chosen (Model və seçim səbəbi)

The review asked how the wage forecast reaches household income, so income is built as a decomposition that closes as an identity — wage bill (wage × employees) plus transfers plus other income, each leg on its own driver — and raced against the earlier rule that tied income growth directly to non-oil growth. The decomposition won on both constructions and carries the central path; the losing rule is still published as an alternative track. The wage equation with step-year dummies fits the history well but is not used for forecasting, because the timing of future administrative decisions is not in this data set; an ensemble blind to the step years carries the path instead. For the sector wage shares the panel-built composition model beats the untested trend rule but does not clear the random walk, so the benchmark keeps the path and the skill is reported as zero.

Unemployment is modelled in first differences because the level test cannot settle the question on a short window. One transfers equation is insignificant over the full sample and is kept anyway, because it beats the benchmark in the expanding-window test — the test decides, not the t-statistic.

#7Reading (Şərh)

Real income growth is close to flat across the horizon at about 5.1 %, carried by legs that move in proportion to their weight: the wage bill reaches 65,015 mln AZN and other income 67,789 mln AZN by the final year. The unemployment rate barely moves, which reflects the thinness of the Okun link rather than a firm result. The 80% band on income growth contains both alternative tracks.

Two negative results belong here. Building the aggregate wage from nineteen activity-level equations scored below the random walk, so that candidate was dropped; the activity panel earns its place in the wage-bill leg instead, where the sector construction wins. And all four sector wage shares are carried by the benchmark, so their skill is zero.
