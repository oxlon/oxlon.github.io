Trade and payments structure

FR11 · notebook FR11.ipynb · statutory sheet 2.4.1.13.-14.

#Requirement (Tələb)

AZ, verbatim (MİİS TT §15.5.1, FR11): "Ticarət və tədiyə balansına görə idxal-ixracın əsas göstəriciləri, struktur təhlili və proqnozlaşdırılması."

English: "Analysis and forecasting of the main export-import indicators and their structure on the trade and payments balance basis."

Source: MİİS_TT_.pdf, pp. 36–40. Legal basis: Cabinet of Ministers decision No. 75 of 24.05.2004, clause 2.4.1.

This requirement takes FR7's flows one step further: composite balances for the oil-gas and non-oil blocks, the non-oil coverage ratio and export concentration, and the income items without which the current account cannot be completed.

#Model and why it was chosen (Model və seçim səbəbi)

Most of this page is arithmetic on paths that already exist: the composite balances and the coverage ratio are identities over FR7's four flows, and the concentration series are descriptive, held at their last actual value with the short sample stated rather than fitted with a trend. The one new behavioural equation is net primary income, driven by the value of oil-gas exports because profit distributions under production-sharing follow export revenue; two variants, with and without a dummy for the devaluation years, are both kept and the test decides their weights. Secondary income gets no structural equation: the series is small, changes sign and attaches to no macro driver, so it is calibrated on a five-year mean and presented as a benchmark treatment.

The devaluation dummy is large and significant; the notebook records it as an observation about cost-recovery and profit-oil terms under falling prices, not as a model of those contracts, so the equation is a reduced form.

#Reading (Şərh)

The structure is unchanged over the horizon: an oil-gas surplus of 18,025 mln USD financing a non-oil deficit that widens to -16,421 mln USD as domestic absorption grows, with the coverage ratio close to flat at 22.2 %. The net primary income deficit narrows, because investor payments fall with export value while portfolio income rises with the reserve stock. The distance from the template sample follows from the oil-gas export path, not from a different income model.

Three limitations stay on the record. The repatriation definition awaits confirmation, which is why both candidate rows are published side by side. The instrument split is held at its three-year average share, because the sub-lines have no separate driver. And the concentration block is too short to forecast, so it is held constant with no band — a continuity assumption, not an econometric result.
