GDP: real growth, deflator, sector and ownership structure

FR1 · notebooks FR01.ipynb and FR05b.ipynb · statutory sheet 2.4.1.1.

#Requirement (Tələb)

AZ, verbatim (MİİS TT §15.5.1, FR1): "ÜDM-in real artım, deflyator indeksi, dövlət və qeyri-dövlət sektorunun, neft və qeyri-neft sektorunun payı, onların artım və deflyator indeksləri tempinin proqnozlaşdırılması."

English: "Forecasting the real growth rate and the deflator index of GDP, the shares of the state and non-state sectors and of the oil and non-oil sectors, and the growth and deflator indices of each."

Source: MİİS_TT_.pdf, pp. 36–40. Legal basis: Cabinet of Ministers decision No. 75 of 24.05.2004, clause 2.4.1.

One sentence, three quantity groups: the aggregate, the oil/non-oil split, the ownership split.

#Model and why it was chosen (Model və seçim səbəbi)

The direction was inverted in this release: the headline non-oil path is FR2's bottom-up production sum plus the oil-gas block plus the net-tax split, not a top-down demand equation with the sectors scaled onto it. The demand equation survives as the expenditure-side reading and the scenario engine feeding FR6 and FR8.

The race is published both ways: bottom-up +56% against the random walk, demand core +46%. Neither is scaled onto the other; the difference is published as its own series and breaches the internal tolerance in most forecast years. Aggregate GDP carries no equation.

#Oil-gas block: volume × price (FR05b)

Oil-gas growth was a percentage copied from the production plan, with a Brent-only deflator and a 2022 dummy. Real growth is now a Laspeyres volume index over marketable oil and gas at the previous year's export prices, and the price channel an export-value-weighted chained oil-and-gas index, which carries the 2022 gas episode directly — so the dummy loses significance. Forward volumes remain the Ministry's plan.

#Reading (Şərh)

Non-oil growth carries the aggregate: the oil-gas volume path is flat to slightly negative, so GDP growth sits below non-oil growth every year and the oil-gas share falls. The path runs above both the template-sample scenario and the IMF reference for a measurable reason — the sector drivers behind the bottom-up sum do not settle onto a constant rate, while both references do. The two readings of non-oil GDP diverge with the horizon; the difference is published, not reconciled.

#Backtest (Geriyə Sınaq)

Rows from validation_backtest.csv: nonoil_realg, nonoil_realg_asagidan_yuxari, oil_defl, nonoil_defl, inv_total_g, private_share_gdp, oil_realg — losing models included, with ensemble weights.

Reading: the oil-gas volume identity is the strongest single model here (+59%; ensemble +68%). Two weak results stand — the private-share AR(1) barely improves on the benchmark (+6%), and the demand core's advantage depends on a window excluding 2016; with that year in, almost none of it survives.

#Reproduce (Təkrar istehsal)

cd 14August_Macroeconomic_Model_v2
python3 run_all.py --stage FR05b   # oil-gas block: volumes, prices, oil_realg
python3 run_all.py --stage FR01    # GDP core, deflators, identities
python3 run_all.py                 # full DAG — required for the headline

FR01 appears twice in the DAG: the first pass solves the core and writes the keys FR02 needs, the second assembles the headline from FR02's aggregate. A single --stage FR01 run gives the core, not the headline.
