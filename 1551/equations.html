Equation catalogue

Every equation the module estimates, and every equation the Ministry submitted — in one place, numbered and addressable.

#1Scope

Two catalogues sit here side by side, and they are not the same thing.

The first is ours: the 386 rows of 2_neticeler/equations_catalog.csv that belong to requirements FR1–FR12, grouped by requirement and numbered E-FRn.k in the catalogue's own order. Every fitted equation shown on a requirement page carries that number in its margin and links to its entry here, where the estimation sample, the adjusted fit and the backtest record are recorded. Where the frozen notebook extract holds the rendered form, the fitted equation is displayed as the notebook printed it, coefficients and standard errors included; where it does not, the entry gives the estimated quantity and the terms it was estimated on, exactly as the catalogue records them. No entry is retyped from a notebook by hand. Each entry's description is the catalogue's own Azerbaijani sentence, quoted rather than translated so that no entry states anything the contract file does not; the classification the notebook gave the row (structural, benchmark, robustness check, comparison) is shown beside it in English.

The second is the department's own, and it is an addition (əlavə) rather than a requirement: §15.5.1 asks for FR1–FR12 and says nothing about whose equations must be used. It holds the 92 equations of the Ministry specification, shown with their original names and their original EViews right-hand sides, each carrying the status the solver reached with it. 91 of the 92 parse. 81 run live — 66 exactly as submitted, 14 on documented substitutes and 1 on a flagged error-correction equivalence. 7 wait on error-correction levels the specification does not define, 3 on variables it does not define, and 1 is prose rather than syntax. That is the 66 + 14 + 1 / 7 / 3 / 1 breakdown, and it is read from the catalogue at build time rather than stated from memory.

The two are never merged. Ours is the module's forecast; the Ministry's is published beside it under its own source label as a fourth scenario, and the comparison of the two is on the Ministry specification page, which is listed under additions, not under the requirements. The register records which requirement each equation serves; coverage is checked in the requirement register.

Source files: equations_catalog.csv for both catalogues, validation_backtest.csv for the skill chips, and a frozen extract of the notebooks' own rendered output for the equation forms quoted verbatim on this page.
