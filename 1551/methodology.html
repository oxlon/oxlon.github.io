Method

#1The execution chain

The module is one directed pipeline, not a folder of independent spreadsheets. The oil price is estimated first, then the exchange rate, then the oil-gas volume-and-price block, then consumer prices; those four write the driver paths that everything downstream consumes. The GDP core runs next and solves the simultaneous non-oil block (growth, investment, consumption, income) by year-by-year fixed-point iteration. The eleven-sector production block then builds non-oil value added from the bottom up, and the GDP notebook runs a second time to take the headline aggregate from that sum rather than from its own demand equation — the inversion the Ministry's review asked for. Branch structure, product volumes, investment and household income follow in a parallel group, then trade, then the balance-of-payments structure and closure, and last the Ministry-specification scenario, which reads every upstream output. That is 15 stages over 14 notebooks; the GDP notebook appears twice because its core does not depend on the production block, so the two passes write identical keys and the pipeline closes exactly.

#2Five disciplines

#1. The backtest gate

Every behavioural equation is raced against a random walk on an expanding, one-step-ahead, out-of-sample window — Geriyə Sınaq (backtest) — and all candidates for one series are scored on the same window, so the comparison is fair. Skill is reported as

skill=1−RMSEhmodelRMSEhRW\text{skill} = 1 - \frac{\mathrm{RMSE}^{\text{model}}_h}{\mathrm{RMSE}^{\mathrm{RW}}_h}skill=1−RMSEhRW​RMSEhmodel​​

and an equation that cannot beat the benchmark does not carry the published path. Losers are published rather than deleted: the ledger holds 6,709 rows across 168 series and 111 model labels, and it includes the fixed-share investment rule at −73% and every oil-gas goods-import candidate, none of which beat the benchmark.

#2. The identity chain

Aggregates are built by identity and the identities are asserted in every year, not only in the base year: output equals intermediate consumption plus value added by activity, the eleven activities plus net taxes equal GDP, oil plus non-oil equals the total, and the net-tax split parameter stays inside its admissible range. The production block's verifier runs 108 such checks and the GDP block's 29, each printing the maximum residual it found. Where two independent readings of the same quantity disagree, the difference is published as its own series instead of being reconciled away.

#3. The look-ahead guard

Estimation samples end at 2,025 centrally, in configuration, never by a slice inside a notebook; the forecast horizon is 2,026–2,030. No regression may see a value from the Ministry's own forecast columns — the data layer keeps actuals and overlay paths in separate objects — and each estimated equation carries an explicit no-look-ahead check in its notebook's verifier. Three test modules guard the contract: 36 loader checks, 8 output-contract checks and 9 named econometric suites.

#4. One assumption set

Every forecast-horizon input enters through a single file, assumptions.csv, written by the upstream stages in DAG order: 63 keys × five years = 315 rows. Nothing in the horizon is typed in by hand, and the IMF reference paths are structurally barred from becoming inputs. Because the file is the only channel, an official path can replace a modelled one by substituting a key, without touching an equation.

#5. The dynamics metric

The review's charge that sector forecasts were too flat is answered with a measurement rather than an assertion. For each series the module publishes

dynamism(s)=σ(forecast over the horizon)σ(actual over the estimation window)\text{dynamism}(s)=\frac{\sigma\big(\text{forecast over the horizon}\big)}{\sigma\big(\text{actual over the estimation window}\big)}dynamism(s)=σ(actual over the estimation window)σ(forecast over the horizon)​

as 43 rows in the backtest ledger. The result is not flattering everywhere and is printed as found: the production block's own acceptance check reports 1/24 series inside the internal band and the GDP block 1/7, with a recorded reason for each series outside it.
