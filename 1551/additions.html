What was added

Work delivered beyond the twelve functional requirements of §15.5.1, kept on its own page so that it is never counted as part of them

#1Why this page exists

The specification states twelve functional requirements. Those twelve are the requirement pages, and they are the only thing the register scores. Ten further pieces of work were delivered that the twelve sentences do not ask for — some because a review item asked for them, some because a requirement could not be served honestly without them — and they are listed here rather than mixed into the requirement list, so that nothing on this site is presented as a requirement that is not one. Each row states what the addition is, what it produced as a measured result, and where it can be read or opened. The sequence below is the delivery order, not a ranking.

#3Data collected by us

Several requirements could not be served from the files supplied with them. Where that happened the data was obtained from published sources, provenanced per series rather than per file, and recorded with its retrieval date. The last column is the point of the table: collecting data is not itself a result, and each row states what the collection changed in the published numbers — including where it changed them for the worse.
